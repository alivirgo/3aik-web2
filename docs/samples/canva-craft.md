## Body

The Verge reported on Thursday that Canva has shipped Canva Craft, and that this is the first image model the company built itself.

That order of operations is the part worth keeping. The Verge says Craft works in two steps. It settles the composition. Then it renders the picture. Canva's announcement, as The Verge relayed it, presents that sequence as a bid for tighter and more repeatable results. The piece does not include a public scorecard, so this desk will not invent one.

Resolution is specific. The Verge says output can reach 2K, and that the tool offers more than 30 frame shapes. Those are production details, not a mood. A square post, a story frame, and a wide banner do not share a crop. A system that can switch crops is aimed at design software, which is the shelf Canva already sells.

The Verge also sets Craft beside image tools from OpenAI, Microsoft, and Google. The report does not say Canva has beaten them. It says the new model is competing for the same sort of job. Those are different claims. One is a launch. The other would be a measured result, and no such result is in the story used here.

What does a layout-first pass mean, in plain shop terms? A lot of image tools take a sentence and return a finished picture in one go. If the crop is wrong, you write another sentence and hope. A planner that commits to the composition first can, in principle, hold the empty space and the hierarchy still while the pixels arrive. The Verge attributes that intention to Canva's announcement. It does not show the planner's intermediate file, and neither will this article.

Who feels this first? People who already work inside Canva, if the feature is switched on for them. The Verge story, as read for this piece, does not spell out the price, the region list, or the date each account type gets access. Those blanks stay blank on purpose.

There is a second question under the launch. Design software used to sell templates, stock, and a familiar editor. An in-house model pulls generation onto the same shelf. That can make a slide faster. It can also nudge a lot of social posts toward the same smooth finish, because the defaults travel with the tool. The Verge does not claim that sameness is already happening. It is a risk that sits next to any default generator, and it is fair to name the risk without dressing it up as a finding.

Keep the sourcing tight. The name is Canva Craft. The outlet is The Verge. The reported mechanics are a layout step, then an image step, with a ceiling of 2K and more than 30 frame shapes, positioned near the image tools of three large software companies. Anything past that is not this story.

Until Canva or The Verge publishes the price, the access list, and a side by side of the layout file against the finished picture, any claim of a win over other tools is a story this desk refuses to write.

Does the planner actually hold a layout still once type, product photos, and brand colors enter the file? The Verge does not answer that. Until a later report does, the honest summary is narrower: Canva is now building its own image model, and it says the model thinks about the design before it paints.
