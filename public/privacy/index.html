1. Who this policy covers

NUC7 Studios operates 3aik and the 3aikGPT Android application. This policy covers the hosted workspace at 3aik.com, its API, the Android WebView client, and the official command-line and desktop clients. A model endpoint that you configure yourself has its own operator and privacy terms.

2. Information processed

Content you choose to submit

When you send a request, 3aik processes the prompt, selected attachments, relevant recent conversation context, mode settings, and optional custom instructions. Coding-agent requests can also contain file excerpts, command output, and diffs produced by tools you approve on your computer.

Data kept on your device

The browser and Android client can store threads, drafts, preferences, attachments, generated images, and the offline application shell in browser storage. The desktop and CLI clients store settings and a random device identifier locally. Workspace files changed by the coding agent remain wherever you selected them.

Service and security data

Requests inherently expose network information such as IP address, user agent, time, and route to Cloudflare. A random x-3aik-device identifier supports rate limiting and abuse prevention; it is not an account or a cross-device identity. The download counter reads aggregate public GitHub Release totals and does not inspect prompts.

3. Why information is used

We use submitted content to return the requested output; local records to restore your workspace; and limited request metadata and device identifiers to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the service. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these activities rely on your request or consent, performance of the service you asked for, NUC7 Studios’ legitimate interest in a secure and reliable service, and legal obligations.

3aik does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal data, or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. AI providers and transfers

3aik Cloud

The hosted workspace sends inference requests through a Cloudflare Worker to Cloudflare Workers AI. The 3aik application does not write conversation payloads to a database, object store, or account profile. Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train its AI models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent. See Cloudflare’s Workers AI data-usage notice.

Cloudflare processes service traffic on a global network. Operational and security metadata may be handled or retained under Cloudflare’s agreements, settings, and privacy documentation, including across borders. Do not submit secrets, regulated data, or information you lack permission to process.

Local-model clients

Ollama, LM Studio, and llama.cpp loopback presets send model traffic to software running on your device. A custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint may be on your local network or the internet. The CLI and desktop app send prompts and tool results only to the endpoint you select and do not silently fall back to 3aik Cloud; the selected endpoint’s operator can see that content.

External links and downloads

GitHub hosts source code and release downloads. Links opened outside 3aik, including links from model output, are governed by the destination’s policy. The Android app asks before opening an external destination.

5. Retention and security

Remembered browser data stays on the device until you delete it, disable remembered conversations, clear app/browser storage, or uninstall the application. The random device identifier remains until the relevant client data is reset. 3aik does not maintain a server-side conversation archive, so it cannot restore a deleted local thread.

We use HTTPS, restrictive browser security headers, bounded requests, abuse controls, and approval boundaries in coding clients. No system is risk-free. AI output can be inaccurate, and local tool access can affect files after your approval.

6. Delete or export your data

  • Turn off Remember conversations to remove durable browser history and media while keeping the current session in memory.
  • Delete an individual thread, clear all threads, or export/import a workspace JSON backup from Settings.
  • On Android, use Privacy & local data → Clear local app data, Android’s Clear storage control, or uninstall the app.
  • Remove CLI or desktop settings through the client, and delete any workspace files or exported backups yourself.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Because 3aik has no accounts or server-side conversation history, we may need enough request context to locate any operational record and may not be able to link anonymous traffic to you.

7. Children

3aik is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information should contact us.

8. Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means the updated policy applies to later processing.
